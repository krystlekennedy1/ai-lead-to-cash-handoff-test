What's driving it
Kickoff scheduling is the biggest single wait. It averages 3.9 days and is nearly identical across SMB, Commercial and Enterprise (3.8 to 4.0), with a floor of 2 days. That pattern points to a process gap rather than a deal problem: kickoff seems to be scheduled only after the invoice goes out. Capping the wait at 2 days would cut the average total from 9.9 to about 8.0 days.
Credit checks are slow on large deals. Deals of $100K or more average 4.4 days in credit, and 38% take 5 days or more. Deals under $100K average 1.6 days, and only 1% take 5 or more. Enterprise averages 4.7 days in credit versus 1.6 for SMB. Capping credit at 3 days would save about 0.7 days on average, concentrated on the biggest deals.
Nonstandard terms add about 4 days. They average 13.3 days total versus 9.0 for standard terms, and the gap holds in every segment. Legal itself takes 3.8 days. Legal and credit also run in sequence, so credit doesn't start until legal finishes. Nonstandard deals average 7.0 days from review to credit sign-off, versus 2.2 days of credit alone on standard deals. Starting credit alongside legal is the clearest fix here.
The two factors compound. 68% of large nonstandard deals take 14 days or more, versus 3% of small standard ones. Across the board, 66% of the 47 deals that took 14 or more days had nonstandard terms, and 74% were $100K or more. They are your slow lane.

What isn't the problem
Rework count has almost no relationship to cycle time (correlation 0.01), so I would not prioritize it on speed grounds. It may still matter for quality or cost.
Order review (0.7 days) and booking (0.6 days) are fine. Invoicing is a small gap at about 1.5 days.
Cycle time has been flat from July through September.

What I'd fix, in order
Trigger kickoff scheduling at booking or credit approval instead of after invoicing, with a 2-day target.
Run credit in parallel with legal for nonstandard deals.
Set a credit SLA of 3 days for deals of $100K or more, with a fast-track path for Enterprise.
Give nonstandard-terms deals a legal intake checklist or pre-approved clause library to shorten the 3.8-day legal step.
